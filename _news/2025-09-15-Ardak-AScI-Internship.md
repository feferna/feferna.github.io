title: "Ardak Alipova completed her Aalto Science Institute (AScI) internship under my supervision"
date: 2025-11-15
---

I am happy to share that I have successfully supervise my first student: **[Ardak Alipova](https://kz.linkedin.com/in/ardaq) from the Nazarbayev University (NU) in Kazakhstan!**

Alipova worked as a summer intern for the [Aalto Science Institute (AScI)](https://www.aalto.fi/en/aalto-science-institute-asci) during the 2025 summer (from June to September). We worked together in a paper submitted to the 2026 ACM CHI conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems.

We worked at the intersection of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, and **Bayesian and design optimization**.

Wishing you all the best in your future endeavors, Ardak!
